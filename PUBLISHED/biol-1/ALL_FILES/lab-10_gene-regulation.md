# Lab 10: Gene Regulation & Epigenetics

## Overview

How do cells decide which genes to turn on or off? In this lab, you will explore models of gene regulation, including the *lac* operon and epigenetic modifications.

## Learning Objectives

1. Model the induction and repression of the *lac* operon.
2. Simulate the effect of DNA methylation and histone acetylation on gene accessibility.
3. Investigate environmental influences on gene expression.

## Activity

* **Operon Simulation:** Role-play the parts of the *lac* operon (repressor, promoter, RNA polymerase) with and without lactose.
* **Epigenetics Model:** Use varying tightness of "DNA" winding (yarn/string) to represent chromatin states and access for transcription.
