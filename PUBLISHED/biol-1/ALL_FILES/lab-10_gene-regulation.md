# Lab 10: Gene Regulation & Epigenetics

## Overview

All cells in your body have the same DNA, yet a neuron looks and acts very differently from a skin cell. This is due to **gene regulation**: the ability to turn specific genes on or off. In this lab, you will explore two mechanisms of regulation: the *lac* operon (in bacteria) and epigenetic modification (in eukaryotes).

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lab, you will be able to:

1. Model the components of the *lac* operon and explain inducible gene regulation.
2. Demonstrate how repressors block transcription.
3. Simulate epigenetic modifications (methylation, acetylation) using physical models.
4. Explain how environmental factors (diet, stress) can influence gene expression.

## Conclusion

Gene regulation allows cells to be efficient and specialized. Operons control prokaryotic genes in response to the environment, while chromatin structure controls eukaryotic access to DNA.
