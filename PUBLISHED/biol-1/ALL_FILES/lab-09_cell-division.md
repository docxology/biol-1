# Lab 9: Cell Division — Mitosis & Meiosis

## Overview

This lab focuses on the mechanisms of cell division. You will observe cells in various stages of mitosis under a microscope (or using prepared images) and model the chromosomal movements of meiosis to understand genetic diversity.

## Learning Objectives

1. Identify the phases of mitosis (Prophase, Metaphase, Anaphase, Telophase) in onion root tip cells.
2. Model the events of meiosis (crossing over, independent assortment) using chromosome kits (e.g., pop beads).
3. Compare the products of mitosis vs. meiosis (2 identical vs. 4 unique cells).

## Activity

* **Onion Root Tip Lab:** Count cells in each phase of mitosis to estimate the relative duration of each phase.
* **Meiosis Modeling:** Use bead models to simulate crossing over and independent assortment, tracking alleles to gametes.
