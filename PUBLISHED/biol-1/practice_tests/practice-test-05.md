B) Incomplete dominance

**9.** In codominance, the heterozygote shows:

A) Only the dominant trait

A) Yes, always  
B) Yes, sometimes

**17.** A polygenic trait is controlled by:

A) One gene  
B) Two alleles  
C) Many genes

**18.** Human height is an example of:

A) Simple dominant/recessive inheritance  
B) Codominance  
C) Polygenic inheritance

**19.** Mendel's Law of Segregation states that:

A) Genes on different chromosomes sort independently

**20.** Mendel's Law of Independent Assortment applies to genes that are:

A) On the same chromosome
