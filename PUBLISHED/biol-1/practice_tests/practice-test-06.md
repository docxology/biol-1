## Part A: Multiple Choice (25 questions)

*Choose the best answer for each question.*

**1.** Gene expression means:

A) Copying DNA  
B) Using a gene to make its protein product  
C) Mutating a gene  
D) Deleting a gene

**2.** All cells in your body have the same DNA. Different cell types exist because:

A) They have different DNA  
B) They express different genes  
C) They mutate during development  
D) They lose unnecessary chromosomes

**3.** A transcription factor is a protein that:

A) Copies DNA  
B) Translates mRNA  
C) Helps turn a gene on or off  
D) Destroys mRNA

**4.** A promoter is:

A) An enzyme that builds proteins  
B) A DNA sequence where RNA polymerase binds to start transcription  
C) A type of RNA  
D) A histone modification

**5.** An operon is:

A) A group of genes controlled by one switch  
B) A single gene with many exons  
C) A type of mutation  
D) A eukaryotic structure

**6.** In the lac operon, when lactose is ABSENT:

A) The genes are turned on  
B) The repressor binds to the operator and blocks transcription  
C) RNA polymerase makes enzymes  
D) The repressor is inactive

**7.** In the lac operon, when lactose is PRESENT:

A) The repressor stays on the operator  
B) The genes remain off  
C) Lactose changes the repressor's shape so it falls off, and genes turn on  
D) The promoter is destroyed

**8.** The lac operon is an example of:

A) Constitutive expression  
B) Repressible regulation  
C) Inducible regulation  
D) Epigenetic silencing

**9.** Epigenetics involves changes in gene expression that:

A) Change the DNA sequence  
B) Do NOT change the DNA sequence  
C) Only occur in bacteria  
D) Are always permanent

**10.** DNA methylation typically:

A) Turns genes on  
B) Turns genes off (silences them)  
C) Has no effect  
D) Deletes genes

**11.** Histone acetylation:

A) Tightens DNA around histones  
B) Loosens DNA, making genes accessible (turns them on)  
C) Destroys histones  
D) Blocks translation

**12.** Euchromatin is:

A) Tightly packed; genes are off  
B) Loosely packed; genes can be read  
C) Found only in bacteria  
D) A type of mutation

**13.** Heterochromatin is:

A) Loosely packed; genes are active  
B) Tightly packed; genes are silenced  
C) Only found during mitosis  
D) The same as euchromatin

**14.** X-inactivation is:

A) A mutation on the X chromosome  
B) The random shutdown of one X chromosome in female cells  
C) The deletion of the X chromosome  
D) Only found in males

**15.** A Barr body is:

A) An active X chromosome  
B) A condensed, inactivated X chromosome  
C) A type of ribosome  
D) A bacterial structure

**16.** Calico cats demonstrate X-inactivation because:

A) They have mutations in coat color genes  
B) Different X chromosomes are active in different patches of skin cells  
C) They are always male  
D) Their genes are not regulated

**17.** Alternative splicing allows:

A) One gene to make multiple different proteins  
B) DNA to be replicated faster  
C) Mutations to be corrected  
D) tRNA to carry more amino acids

**18.** miRNA functions by:

A) Activating transcription  
B) Blocking translation of specific mRNAs  
C) Replicating DNA  
D) Building ribosomes

**19.** Which environmental factor can affect gene expression through epigenetics?

A) Diet  
B) Stress  
C) Toxins  
D) All of the above

**20.** Identical twins may develop different traits over time because of:

A) Different DNA sequences  
B) Different epigenetic patterns accumulating over their lifetimes  
C) Different numbers of chromosomes  
D) Mutations in every cell

**21.** The most common and energy-efficient level of gene regulation is:

A) Post-translational  
B) Translational  
C) Transcriptional  
D) Chromatin level

**22.** A housekeeping gene is:

A) A gene that is only expressed in the liver  
B) A gene that is expressed in all cells at all times  
C) A gene involved in cell division  
D) A gene that is always silenced

**23.** A tumor suppressor gene that is silenced by heavy methylation (but not mutated) could:

A) Have no effect on the cell  
B) Contribute to cancer by allowing uncontrolled growth  
C) Make the cell divide more slowly  
D) Turn the cell into a nerve cell

**24.** The key difference between epigenetics and a mutation is:

A) Epigenetics changes the DNA sequence; mutations don't  
B) Mutations change the DNA sequence; epigenetics changes expression without changing the sequence  
C) They are the same thing  
D) Epigenetics only affects bacteria

**25.** An enhancer is a DNA sequence that:

A) Silences a gene  
B) Boosts transcription of a gene, even from far away  
C) Codes for a protein  
D) Serves as a start codon
