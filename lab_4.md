Part 1 -- Diffusion on Agar plate.
Potassium Permanganate (KMnO4) dye
Methylene Blue dye
One of these molecules is twice as large as the other.
Put drop of each dye in the hole in center of agar plate, and let it diffuse.
Check back at 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60 minutes with the radius of diffusion (measure with ruler)

Part 2 -- Diffusion in water
Heating plate.
There is Hot water (from hot plate) and ice water (ice machine in chemical room in corner)
Have a hot and cold water in separate beakers.
With the Red dye, add one drop of red dye to the hot water
With the Blue dye, add one drop of blue dye to the cold water
Watch it diffuse (Hot water diffuses faster.)
Stir both Red and Blue to be sure they are well mixed.
Prepare the plastic frame with the plastic vertical divider in the middle..
Pour the Red on one side, and Blue on the other side (ensure the divider is firmly in place)
Then when you pull the divider -- the blue flows down and to the side, you can see the red on top and blue on bottom.
Have a white background to see better.
Then, while the red and blue are still visually stratified -- place the divider back in the middle, and stir one side.
Now one side is stratified, and the other side is mixed (medium purple)
Remove the divider, and watch how the purple flows to the side, in the middle (between hot and cold)
