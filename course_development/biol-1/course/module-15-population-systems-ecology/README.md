# Module 15: Population and Systems Ecology

Welcome to Module 15! We are now zooming out from species to study the entire biological system. Ecology is the study of how organisms interact with each other and their physical environments. We will map mathematically how populations grow, how they inevitably hit limits, and how grand networks of energy and nutrients sustain the entire globe.

## Module Structure

1. **[Keys to Success](keys-to-success.md)**
   - Understand the difference between exponential (J-curve) and logistic (S-curve) growth, carrying capacity, trophic levels, and the undeniable laws of energy transfer (the 10% rule).
2. **[Practice Questions](questions.md)**
   - Test your ability to read population graphs and calculate energy flow through a food web.

## Learning Objectives

By completing this module, you should be able to:
- Diagram and distinguish between exponential and logistic population growth models.
- Define carrying capacity (K) and identify factors that limit population growth.
- Trace the path of energy through an ecosystem via trophic levels (Producers to apex Predators).
- Apply the 10% Rule of Energy Transfer.
- Briefly explain how vital chemical elements (like Carbon and Nitrogen) cycle through the biosphere.
