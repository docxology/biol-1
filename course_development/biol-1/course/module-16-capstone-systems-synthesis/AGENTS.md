## Module Scope

Capstone synthesis module for BIOL-1 (non-majors). Content explicitly integrates
concepts from across the course:

- Molecular biology (Modules 02–03, 07): how molecular changes propagate upward
- Cell biology (Modules 04–06, 08): cells as the bridge from molecules to organisms
- Genetics (Modules 09–10): heritable variation as the substrate for change
- Evolution (Modules 12–14): populations and species across time
- Ecology (Module 15): organisms in environmental context

**Capstone skills:** mechanism chains across scales, feedback and constraint
identification, evidence-type matching, and synthesis explanation that is
testable and revisable.

**Excluded:** advanced network theory, mathematical modeling of feedback loops,
multi-level regulation details. These remain outside the active BIOL-1 Fall 2026 scope.
