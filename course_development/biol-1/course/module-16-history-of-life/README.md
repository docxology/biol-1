# Module 16: History of Life

Welcome to Module 16, our final module! We end the course by zooming all the way out to observe the entire 4.6 billion-year history of Earth. We will cover the massive milestones of life—from the first primordial cells to the explosive radiations of animals. More importantly, you will learn to read the "family tree" of life (Phylogeny), tracing the evolutionary relationships connecting everything from bacteria to humans.

## Module Structure

1. **[Keys to Success](keys-to-success.md)**
   - Digest the geologic time scale, understand the reset-button nature of mass extinctions, and learn the rules of reading a phylogenetic tree (cladogram).
2. **[Practice Questions](questions.md)**
   - Test your ability to identify key historical biological events and accurately interpret common ancestry from a cladogram.

## Learning Objectives

By completing this module, you should be able to:
- Place major biological milestones (first prokaryotes, oxygenification, first eukaryotes, multicellular life, land colonization) in chronological order.
- Explain the relationship between Mass Extinctions and Adaptive Radiations.
- Correctly read and interpret a phylogenetic tree to determine evolutionary relatedness.
- Define a "clade" and identify shared derived characters.
