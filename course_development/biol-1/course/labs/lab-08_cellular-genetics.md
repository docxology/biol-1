# Lab 8: Cellular Genetics — Mitosis & Meiosis

## Overview

Cells must divide for an organism to grow, to repair damaged tissues, and to reproduce. Eukaryotic cells have two fundamental methods of division: **Mitosis** (for creating identical diploid somatic cells) and **Meiosis** (for creating genetically unique haploid gametes). In this comprehensive lab, you will compare these two processes. You will calculate the relative time cells spend in each mitotic phase by observing real tissue, diagram the critical stages of division, and model how meiosis generates the raw material for evolution: genetic diversity.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lab, you will be able to:

1. Identify the phases of the cell cycle (Interphase, Prophase, Metaphase, Anaphase, Telophase/Cytokinesis) in actively dividing plant and animal cells.
2. Calculate the approximate duration of each mitotic phase based on cell counts from a tissue sample.
3. Model the physical movement of chromosomes during Meiosis I and Meiosis II.
4. Demonstrate how *crossing over* and *independent assortment* create unique genetic combinations.
5. Contrast the biological purposes and outcomes of mitosis (2 diploid clones) versus meiosis (4 haploid unique cells).

## Part 2: Meiosis and Genetic Diversity

While mitosis produces identical clones, meiosis is a reduction division that cuts the chromosome number in half (Diploid `2n` → Haploid `n`), returning to the diploid state only upon fertilization. Meiosis introduces immense genetic variety through two specific mechanisms in Meiosis I.

**Procedure:** Use your chromosome kits to model a cell where **2n = 4**. Use two long chromosome models (one Red for maternal, one Blue for paternal) and two short chromosome models (one Red, one Blue).

### 2A. Crossing Over (Prophase I)

1. Put your cell into Interphase and replicate the DNA (create X-shaped sister chromatids).
2. Enter Prophase I. Bring the homologous pairs together to form **tetrads** (Long Red with Long Blue; Short Red with Short Blue).
3. **Simulate Crossing Over:** Physically break off a small bottom piece of the Long Red chromatid and swap it with the exact corresponding bottom piece of the Long Blue chromatid.
