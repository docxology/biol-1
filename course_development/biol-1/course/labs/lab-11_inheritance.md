# Lab 11: Inheritance — Genetics Problems

## Overview

This lab applies Mendelian principles to solve complex genetics problems. You will work through scenarios involving monohybrid and dihybrid crosses, blood typing, and pedigree analysis.

## Learning Objectives

1. Solve genetics problems using Punnett squares and probability rules.
2. Analyze pedigrees to deduce inheritance patterns (autosomal/sex-linked, dominant/recessive).
3. Determining blood type inheritance (ABO system).

## Activity

* **Corn Genetics:** Observe ears of corn representing F2 crosses (e.g., Purple/Yellow, Smooth/Wrinkled) and calculate phenotypic ratios to test Mendel's laws (Chi-square analysis).
* **Blood Typing Simulation:** (Optional/Virtual) Determine blood types of "patients" based on agglutination reactions.
* **Pedigree Challenge:** Solve "who is the carrier?" puzzles based on family trees.
