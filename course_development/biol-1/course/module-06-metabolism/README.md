# Module 06: Metabolism

## Overview

Life runs on energy. This module covers thermodynamic constraints in cells, ATP as the universal energy coupler, enzymes and activation energy, how environmental conditions regulate enzyme function, and the linked pathways of photosynthesis and cellular respiration.

## Topics

1. Energy and thermodynamics
2. ATP as cellular coupling
3. Enzymes and activation energy
4. Regulation by conditions and inhibitors
5. Photosynthesis and respiration as linked pathways

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

1. Apply thermodynamic ideas to living cells.
2. Explain ATP as an energy-coupling molecule.
3. Describe how enzymes change reaction rates.
4. Predict how environmental conditions affect enzyme activity.
5. Connect photosynthesis and cellular respiration in matter and energy flow.
