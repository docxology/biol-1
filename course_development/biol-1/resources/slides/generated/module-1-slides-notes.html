Biology - The Study of Life

Topic sequence

  • What counts as life: Life is recognized by shared properties: cells, energy use, response, reproduction, regulation, and evolution.
  • Science as a way of testing: Scientific claims are tested with observations, comparisons, variables, and repeatable reasoning.
  • Organization from cells to ecosystems: Biological systems are organized across scales from molecules to biosphere.
  • Evidence, variables, and measurement: Data become useful when measurements are tied to a clear question and source of error.
  • How biological explanations improve: Strong explanations connect evidence to a claim and stay open to revision.

Biology - The Study of Life

Terms as evidence handles

  • Cell: Basic unit of life bounded by a membrane.
  • Homeostasis: Regulation that keeps internal conditions within a workable range.
  • Metabolism: All chemical reactions that move matter and energy through life.
  • Hypothesis: Testable explanation for an observed pattern.
  • Variable: Factor that can change in an investigation.
  • Evidence: Observation or measurement used to support or challenge a claim.
