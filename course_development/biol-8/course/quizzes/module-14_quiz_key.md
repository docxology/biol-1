## Part B: Free Response (3 points)

**8. Bacteria vs. viruses (1 point)**
- Bacteria: cells with ribosomes, metabolism; replicate by division; antibiotics can target walls, ribosomes, enzymes.
- Viruses: acellular; need host; replicate via hijacked machinery; lack typical antibiotic targets.

**9. Aseptic technique (1 point)**
- Reduces contamination from skin, air, or surfaces.
- Protects cultures and the investigator; keeps results interpretable for identification or sensitivity testing.

**10. Inflammatory response (1 point)**
- Local response to infection or injury: vasodilation, increased permeability, recruitment of phagocytes.
- Purpose: contain pathogens, clear debris, initiate repair.
