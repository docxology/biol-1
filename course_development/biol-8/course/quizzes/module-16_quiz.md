   B) The population size increases at a constant rate per unit of time.
   C) It typically occurs in environments with limited resources.

3. The maximum number of individuals of a population that an environment can support is called the:
   A) Growth rate
   B) Biotic potential
   C) Carrying capacity
   D) Ecological niche

4. Two species of birds compete for the same nesting sites. One species eventually outcompetes the other, leading to its local extinction. This is an example of:
   A) Resource partitioning
   B) Mutualism
   C) Competitive exclusion
   D) Commensalism

5. A relationship between two species where one benefits and the other is neither helped nor harmed is:
   A) Mutualism
   B) Commensalism
   C) Parasitism
   D) Predation

   A) Primary producers
   B) Primary consumers
   C) Secondary consumers
   D) Tertiary consumers

   A) Most energy is lost as heat.
   B) Consumers are unable to digest all of the prey.
   C) Not all individuals at one level are eaten by the next.
