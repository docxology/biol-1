## Part A: Multiple Choice (7 points)

*Choose the best answer for each question. (1 point each)*

1. DNA replication occurs during which phase of the cell cycle?
   - A) G1
   - B) S phase
   - C) G2
   - D) M phase

2. The correct order of mitosis phases is:
   - A) Metaphase, Prophase, Anaphase, Telophase
   - B) Prophase, Metaphase, Anaphase, Telophase
   - C) Prophase, Anaphase, Metaphase, Telophase
   - D) Anaphase, Telophase, Prophase, Metaphase

3. Mitosis produces ______, while meiosis produces ______.
   - A) 4 diploid cells; 2 haploid cells
   - B) 2 diploid cells; 4 haploid cells
   - C) 2 haploid cells; 4 diploid cells
   - D) 4 haploid cells; 2 diploid cells

4. Crossing over occurs during which stage?
   - A) Prophase of mitosis
   - B) Prophase I of meiosis
   - C) Anaphase II of meiosis
   - D) Metaphase of mitosis

5. Homologous chromosomes separate during:
   - A) Anaphase of mitosis
   - B) Anaphase I of meiosis
   - C) Anaphase II of meiosis
   - D) Telophase of mitosis

6. Nondisjunction can result in:
   - A) Identical twin formation
   - B) Offspring with abnormal chromosome numbers
   - C) Increased crossing over
   - D) Faster cell division

7. Which is a purpose of mitosis but NOT of meiosis?
   - A) Producing gametes
   - B) Increasing genetic variation
   - C) Growth and tissue repair
   - D) Reducing chromosome number
