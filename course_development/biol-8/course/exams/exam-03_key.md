## Part C: Free Response (9 points)

**Grading guide (3 points each)** — award partial credit for accurate partial explanations.

1. **Bone remodeling.** Expect: mechanical stress stimulates bone formation; reduced loading or microgravity leads to loss; osteoblasts build matrix, osteoclasts resorb; Wolff's law = bone adapts to stress.

2. **Muscle contraction.** Expect: myosin heads pull on actin; tropomyosin/troponin regulate binding sites; calcium binds troponin to expose sites; ATP needed for cycling.

3. **Reflex arc.** Expect: receptor → sensory neuron → spinal interneuron (optional) → motor neuron → effector; mention synapses in cord.

4. **Aseptic technique.** Expect: prevent contamination from skin/air; protect investigator; allow meaningful colony interpretation; avoid cross-contamination.

5. **O₂ path.** Expect: air → alveoli → diffusion into pulmonary capillary blood → pulmonary veins → left atrium → left ventricle → aorta → systemic capillaries → tissue fluid/cell; mention hemoglobin loading in lungs and unloading in tissues.
