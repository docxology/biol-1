# Module 14: Pathogens — Study Questions

## Introduction to Pathogens

## Bacteria
4. How do bacteria differ in cell structure from human cells?

## Viruses
7. Describe the basic structure of a virus.
8. Why are viruses considered non-living by most scientists?
9. How do vaccines help prevent severe viral diseases?

## Fungi, Parasites, and Prions
10. How are fungi different from bacteria, and how do they obtain nutrients?
11. What is the difference between a protozoan parasite and a helminth?
12. What are prions, and why are prion diseases particularly difficult to treat?

## Body Defenses
13. What are the body's first-line defenses against pathogens?
14. How does the skin act as a barrier to protect against infection?
15. What is the inflammatory response, and what protective purpose does it serve?
