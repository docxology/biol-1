Chicken Liver Enzyme Activity Lab.

Blue bin (few inches of water)
First prepare the water in the blue bin.
Prepare the glass tube stand.

Fill the glass tube with water, very carefully to not shoot out. Top it off with distilled water.

You will cover the glass tube with your thumb, then dip that tip under water, place the downward-facing tube over the hole in bottom of blue bin.

Prepare the small reaction beaker with stopper, with tube leading to blue bin.

Learn how to read the tube (how much air is in the top of tube)

Add 1 ml of ground chicken liver to the small reaction beaker.
Add 1 ml of Hydrogen Peroxide to the small reaction beaker.
Immediately cap the beaker, so all gas produced goes through the tan tube to the blue bin, you will see air bubbles start to come to the top of the glass tub.

Measurement table -- how many mL of gas at t=0, t=1 minute, and t=5 minutes.
