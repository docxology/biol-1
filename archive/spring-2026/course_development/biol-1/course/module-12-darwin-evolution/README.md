# Module 12: Darwin and Evolution

Welcome to Module 12! In this module, we will explore the foundational concept of biology: evolution. We will trace the historical context of evolutionary thought, follow Charles Darwin on his voyage aboard the HMS Beagle, and break down exactly how natural selection drives the adaptation of organisms to their environments.

## Module Structure

1. **[Keys to Success](keys-to-success.md)**
   - Start here! This documents the critical concepts, vocabulary, and analogies you need to understand natural selection. 
2. **[Practice Questions](questions.md)**
   - Self-assess your understanding of the mechanisms of evolution and the evidence supporting it.

## Learning Objectives

By completing this module, you should be able to:
- Explain what Darwin meant by "descent with modification."
- Describe the logical steps of natural selection (variation, overproduction, differential reproductive success).
- List and explain the primary lines of evidence for evolution (fossils, biogeography, comparative anatomy, and molecular biology).
- Explain why individuals do not evolve, but populations do.
