1. According to Charles Darwin, what is the primary mechanism of evolutionary change?
   A) Use and disuse of traits
   B) Acquired characteristics
   C) Natural selection
   D) Mutation

2. Which of the following is a requirement for **natural selection** to occur?
   A) Genetic variation within the population
   B) Inheritance of traits
   C) Differential reproductive success
   D) All of the above

3. The specific traits that enhance an organism's survival and reproductive success in its environment are called:
   A) Mutations
   B) Adaptations
   C) Gene flow
   D) Genetic drift

4. Structures that have different functions but are inherited from a common ancestor are known as:
   A) Analogous structures
   B) Homologous structures
   C) Vestigial structures
   D) Divergent structures

5. The wings of a butterfly and the wings of a bird have different anatomical structures but serve the same function. These are:
   A) Homologous structures
   B) Vestigial structures
   C) Analogous structures
   D) Convergent structures

6. What is the "raw material" upon which natural selection acts?
   A) Environmental pressure
   B) Genetic variation
   C) Reproductive isolation
   D) Overproduction of offspring

7. Which evidence for evolution provides the most direct and accurate information about ancestral relationships?
   A) Fossil record
   B) Comparative anatomy
   C) DNA sequence comparisons
   D) Embryology
