# Module 10: Inheritance

**OpenStax Connection:** Chapter 8 (Concepts of Biology) / Chapter 9 (Genetics)

## Overview

In this module, we explore **inheritance**—how genetic information is passed from parents to offspring. Building on what we learned about cell division and DNA in Modules 07 and 08, we now ask: *How do specific traits get distributed to offspring?*

We will study the foundational work of **Gregor Mendel**, whose experiments with pea plants revealed the mathematical patterns of heredity. These patterns, now called **Mendelian genetics**, remain the foundation of modern genetics.

Key topics in this module include:

1. **Mendel's Laws**: Law of Segregation and Law of Independent Assortment
2. **Punnett Squares**: Predicting offspring genotypes and phenotypes for monohybrid and dihybrid crosses
3. **Extensions to Mendelism**: Incomplete dominance, codominance, multiple alleles (blood types), polygenic traits
4. **Sex Determination and Sex-Linked Inheritance**: Why some conditions are X-linked and more common in males
5. **Pedigree Analysis**: Reading family inheritance charts

## Learning Goals

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

* Explain Mendel's Law of Segregation and Law of Independent Assortment.
* Use Punnett squares to solve monohybrid and dihybrid crosses.
* Describe incomplete dominance, codominance, and multiple alleles using real examples.
* Explain the inheritance of sex-linked traits and construct relevant Punnett squares.
* Interpret a pedigree chart to determine inheritance patterns and identify carriers.
