
Using Labquest Mini. Vernier. Logger Pro 3.16.2.
Each group has a Lab Quest Mini, which plugs into a laptop with USB.

There are two liquids

- Pure Tap Water
- Lemon Juice

We are going to measure features from each of the two liquids.

After the first lab section on setup and some questions about what/why we are doing the lab -- the body sections are structured by each Measurement device. Some of the devices we only have 1 of, between the two liquids. So just do the measurements you can do with what you have at that moment, till all are done. Each section, there are: What is the measurement, how to do it, what do you expect the difference will be, and then a few questions about the measurement after making.

- pH meter (pH, also there is a Dip pH measurement)
- Conductivity probe (Total Dissolved Solids)
- Temperature probe (also have a mercury thermometer) Degrees C.
- Dissolved Oxygen probe (Dissolved Oxygen)
- Colorimeter (Absorbance) at  430, 470, 565, 635 nm.
- Turbidity probe (Transmittance level).

Final section -- copy the datapoints into a table with 2 columns (one for each liquid) and 6 rows (one for each measurement).

Then we will share between the two groups, and see similarity and any differences.
